# Silsila-e-khayal-
## 📚 Book Descriptions  

### 📝 *Silsila-e-Khayal*  
A collection of **150+ poetries** that journey through the bittersweet shades of life.  
From the sweetness of love, to the silence of heartbreak, to the eternal longing for death —  
this book is a mirror of emotions that are often left unspoken.  
It is for every soul who has *loved, lost, dreamed, and searched for themselves in their own thoughts.*  

---

### 📖 *Fasana-e-Zindagi*  
A poetic reflection of **life’s struggles, passions, and untold stories**.  
This book captures the essence of human existence —  
where happiness meets grief, where hope collides with despair,  
and where every heartbeat narrates its own fable.  

---

### 🕊 *The Eternal Light*  
**A Journey of Ahl-Bait from Hijrah to Karbala**  
A deeply spiritual and historical masterpiece that walks through the sacrifices of the Ahl-Bait.  
It captures their courage, faith, and resilience against all odds —  
a journey that begins with migration (*Hijrah*) and leads to the unforgettable tragedy of **Karbala**.  

---

### 💔 *Mohabbat Ki Inteha* *(Novel)*  
A romantic novel that dives into the **extremes of love**.  
It explores passion, loyalty, heartbreak, and the painful beauty of devotion.  
Every page resonates with the reality that love is not always about finding peace —  
sometimes it is about *losing yourself in the storm of emotions*.  

---

## ✍️ About the Author  

**Ravees Altaf** is a poet and storyteller whose words capture the deepest shades of human emotions.  
His poetry often flows through the realms of **love, heartbreak, solitude, and the eternal longing for peace in death**.  
Each verse he writes is not just a collection of words, but a mirror that reflects life’s hidden truths.  

Through his writings, he gives voice to the **unspoken feelings** of the heart —
